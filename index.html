Background & Vision

Robotics & Embedded

I’m an EEE student at North South University. I like building things that move, and so far that’s meant robots on the Raspberry Pi Pico, Esp328266, with motors, sensors, and a lot of debugging.

Simulation & Firmware

I simulate circuits in MATLAB and Multisim and draw schematics in EasyEDA. For firmware, I write MicroPython in Thonny for the Raspberry Pi Pico and use the Arduino IDE to program the ESP8266.

IEEE Leadership

I joined IEEE NSU Student Branch as a member in October 2025 and I’m now a core volunteer in logistics and operations. I do the same role in WIE and PES, and I’m on the PR and membership development team for IAS.
