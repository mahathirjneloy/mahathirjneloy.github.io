NSU Code of Conduct: rules, fines and penalties

Search any offence to see the penalty.

The North South University (NSU) Code of Conduct lists what students can be fined, suspended or expelled for. Below is every rule in plain words, from smoking and proxy attendance to ID cards, cheating, social media and clubs. Updated 27 Nov 2026.

Each rule shows the penalty for the 1st, 2nd and 3rd time. These are the highest penalties in the code. NSU judges every case and can give less.

What the penalties mean

Verbal warning
An official warning, noted in your file.
Written warning, guardian called
A final warning. Your guardian comes in and you both sign a bond.
Fine
Money you pay. "Up to" means NSU picks the amount, at most that much.
Suspension
You cannot attend or use the campus for a while, for example up to 2 semesters.
Expulsion
You are removed from NSU for good. It shows on your transcript.

If there is a complaint against you

  1. Someone files a written complaint at the Proctor's Office, or by email.
  2. You and your guardian get the charges in writing first.
  3. At the hearing you explain and can bring evidence and witnesses. If you don't come, it goes on without you.
  4. Not happy with the decision? Apply in writing to the Disciplinary Committee Chairperson within 10 days. That decision is final.

Common questions about the NSU Code of Conduct

Is smoking allowed at NSU?

No. Smoking or vaping on campus is a fine of BDT 500 the first time, BDT 3,000 and a written warning the second time, and suspension the third time.

What is the punishment for proxy attendance at NSU?

Giving proxy can bring a fine up to BDT 5,000 and a written warning the first time, then guardian called and suspension, then expulsion. Giving your ID for proxy can bring a fine up to BDT 10,000 and a written warning, then expulsion.

What happens if you are caught cheating at NSU?

The faculty member can give an F for that work or the whole course, or send the case to the Proctor, who decides the penalty.

What gets you expelled from NSU the first time?

For example drinking or taking drugs on campus, renting out your student ID, using firearms or explosives, forging university papers, arson, and trying to damage NSU's reputation.

Can you appeal a disciplinary decision at NSU?

Yes. Apply in writing to the Disciplinary Committee Chairperson within 10 days of the decision notice. The review decision is final.
