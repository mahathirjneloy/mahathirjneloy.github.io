NSU grading system and retake policy

Type your marks, or tap a question.

The North South University (NSU) grading system runs from A (93 and above, 4.0) to F (below 60, 0.0). This page has the full NSU grade scale, a grade checker, and the retake, probation and add or drop rules from the 2026 academic policies. Updated 27 Nov 2026.

NSU retake, probation and add or drop rules

Tap a question.

Can I retake a B+ at NSU?

No. At NSU you can retake a course only if your grade is B or lower. B+, A- and A cannot be retaken.

Which grade counts after a retake at NSU?

Your best grade. A lower grade on the retake does not hurt you.

How many times can I retake a course at NSU?

Three times. A fourth time needs approval from the Vice-Chancellor.

What CGPA puts you on probation at NSU?

When your CGPA is below 2.00. You get 3 terms to bring it back to 2.00. If you can't, you are dismissed in the 4th term. Graduate programs: below 2.75 (2.70 for EMBA).

What is the NSU course drop deadline without a W?

Until the end of week 3. Nothing goes on your record.

When does a drop show W?

From the end of week 3 to the end of week 6 (week 12 in the semester system). After that you cannot drop.

Until when can I add a course at NSU?

Week 1 with your advisor's OK, if there is space. Week 2 also needs the instructor's OK. After that, no adding.

Can I be dropped for missing classes?

Yes. Missing 3 classes in a row can get you dropped. So can never coming in the first 2 weeks without telling the instructor.

What if I just stop going to classes?

You get F in every course. To leave a term properly, withdraw through the Registrar's Office by week 6 (week 12 in the semester system).

Why can't I register for higher courses?

Pass your required 100 level courses by your 3rd term and ENG105 by your 4th term, or other courses stay locked. Failing a required 100 level course twice locks everything except English until you pass it.

How many credits is full time?

At least 12 credits per trimester, or 15 per semester.
