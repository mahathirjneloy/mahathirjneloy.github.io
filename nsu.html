How to calculate CGPA at NSU

Multiply the credits of each course by its grade point, add the results, then divide by the total credits. For example, a 3 credit A (4.0) and a 3 credit B (3.0) give (12 + 9) / 6 = 3.50.

How are retakes counted?

You can retake a course if your grade is B or lower. The best grade counts for CGPA. A course can normally be retaken three times. A fourth retake needs approval from the Vice-Chancellor.

What GPA do I need to reach my target CGPA?

Enter your total credits, credits passed, current CGPA and target CGPA. The calculator shows the average you need in your remaining credits.
