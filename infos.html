Retake rules

B or lowerOnly these grades can be retaken.
Best countsYour best grade in the course is used for CGPA.
3 retakesThe normal limit. A 4th retake needs approval from the Vice-Chancellor.

CGPA and probation (undergraduate)

  • 2.00 or higherGood academic standing.
  • Below 2.00Probation. You get up to three terms to reach 2.00.
  • 4th termIf you still have not reached 2.00, you are dismissed. A dismissed student cannot be readmitted or start again with a new ID, unless the Academic Council approves an exception.

Graduate programs: the passing CGPA is currently 2.75 (2.70 for EMBA), with the same three term limit.

Adding and dropping courses

  • Week 1Add a course with your advisor’s permission, if there is space.
  • Week 2Adding also needs the instructor’s authorization. No adds after this week.
  • Up to end of week 3Drop with nothing on your record.
  • Week 3 to week 6Dropping puts a W on your record.
  • After week 6No more drops.

Semester system: week 12 replaces week 6. Talk to your advisor before adding or dropping.

Do not get stuck

  • Missing three classes in a row can get you dropped from the course.
  • If you never attend in the first two weeks and do not tell the instructor, you may be dropped.
  • Pass all required 100 level courses by your third term, or higher level courses stay locked (English courses are the exception).
  • Fail a required 100 level course twice and you can take only that course and English until you pass it.
  • Pass ENG105 by your fourth term, or other courses stay locked.
  • Leaving a term without officially withdrawing gives you F grades.

Exceptions to the 100 level and English rules need the department Chair and further approval.

More rules

Withdrawing for a whole term
  • Contact the Office of the Registrar. Telling an instructor is not enough.
  • Not allowed after week 6 (week 12 in a semester), except for verified non-academic hardship approved by the department Chair.
Credits and time limits
  • Full time: at least 12 credits per trimester (15 per semester).
  • Taking more than the regular load needs written permission.
  • Finish an undergraduate degree within 6 years and a graduate degree within 4. Exceptions need the Academic Council.
  • Class standing by credits earned: 0 to 30 Freshman, 31 to 60 Sophomore, 61 to 90 Junior, over 90 Senior.
Excluding courses
  • Only after completing 80% of required credits.
  • Core courses cannot be excluded.
  • Not allowed for raising CGPA to avoid probation, change department or get financial aid.
  • After changing program, courses the new program does not require can be excluded.
Transfer, program change, internship
  • Up to 50% of credit earned elsewhere can transfer, only for grade C or above. Transferred grades do not count in CGPA.
  • To change program, in general raise CGPA to 3.00 in 3 terms and complete 27 credits in your current program, then apply to the department Chair.
  • Internship needs at least 100 credits. No other courses during it.
Registration and readmission
  • Late registration needs the department Chair’s permission and a late fee.
  • In good standing but missed two terms in a row without notice? You must apply for readmission.

The penalties

  • Verbal warning: goes in your file.
  • Written warning + guardian called: final warning, you and your guardian sign a bond.
  • Loss of privilege: a service or activity is taken away for a while.
  • Fine, and restitution (paying for damage or loss).
  • Disciplinary probation: you keep studying, but another violation can bring harsher action. Awards or financial aid may be affected.
  • Suspension: for example up to 2 semesters. A temporary one can start at once if someone is seen as a threat, and returning to campus during it can lead to dismissal or expulsion.
  • Expulsion: permanent, needs Vice-Chancellor approval, and shows on your transcript.

If something happens

  • ComplainGo to the Proctor’s Office and file a written complaint on their form, or email the Proctor or an assistant proctor.
  • NoticeThe accused student and guardian are told the charges in writing, in advance.
  • HearingYou can explain and bring evidence and witnesses. Unknown witnesses do not count. If you do not appear, the case can be heard without you.
  • ReviewApply in writing with your reasons within 10 calendar days of the decision notice. The Disciplinary Committee’s review decision is final.
